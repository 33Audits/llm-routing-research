# RouterEval: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Routing LLMs to Explore Model-level Scaling Up in LLMs

- **Authors:** Zhongzhan Huang, Guoming Ling, Yupei Lin, Yandong Chen, Shanshan Zhong, Hefeng Wu, Liang Lin
- **Submitted:** 8 March 2025; current arXiv version v2 revised 20 May 2025
- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10657
- **Code and data:** https://github.com/MilkThink-Lab/RouterEval

## Summary

RouterEval is a large benchmark for evaluating LLM routers. It contains more than 200 million performance records spanning 12 LLM evaluations and more than 8,500 models. The paper studies how routing performance changes as the candidate model pool grows and reports a model-level scaling effect in which a capable router can outperform the strongest individual model in the pool.

## Routing relevance

RouterEval provides a much broader testbed than small fixed model-pool comparisons. It is valuable for measuring router generalization, candidate-pool scaling, and whether improvements come from the routing policy or simply from adding stronger models to the pool.
